# Contributing to QuantWeft / 参与 QuantWeft

Thank you for helping improve the explanations, exercises, translations, code and reproducibility. Read [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md) and [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) before submitting material.

欢迎改进讲解、练习、翻译、代码和可复现性。提交前请阅读 [许可范围](LICENSE.md) 与 [第三方声明](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md)。

## What to submit / 如何提交

Open an issue or a focused pull request describing the reader problem, affected chapter or stable exercise ID, the proposed correction, and how it was checked. Keep code, derivations, units, prerequisites, questions and complete answers consistent. State whether calculations were recomputed, code was run, or only text was reviewed; a successful build alone does not establish teaching or research validity.

通过 issue 或范围明确的 PR 说明读者问题、涉及章节或稳定题号、修改内容和检查方式。同步核对代码、推导、单位、先修、题目和完整答案；区分实际运行、独立复算与纯文字检查。无需为改错重训模型或扩大研究范围。

Do not upload proprietary or personal data, credentials, unlicensed figures, copied textbook passages or exercises without the required permission, or market data with unverified redistribution terms. Cite sources and identify the exact license and any modifications for third-party material. Discuss new dependencies, datasets and major structural changes before adding them.

请勿提交专有或个人数据、认证信息、无权分发的图片、未经所需许可复制的教材段落或习题，以及再分发权利未核实的市场数据。第三方内容须说明出处、具体许可及修改范围；新增依赖、数据或大规模结构调整先讨论。

## Contribution licensing / 贡献许可

By intentionally submitting a contribution for inclusion, you confirm that you have the right to submit it under the applicable project license: MIT for your original software and CC BY-SA 4.0 for your original educational content, with mixed Notebooks split as described in LICENSE.md. Clearly identified third-party components keep their declared licenses and require review. You retain ownership of your contributions; no copyright assignment is requested. Do not submit material subject to incompatible employer, co-author, publisher or data-provider terms.

主动提交供项目收录的贡献时，你确认有权按对应许可提供：原创软件按 MIT，原创教学内容按 CC BY-SA 4.0，混合 Notebook 按根许可说明区分。明确标出的第三方组成部分保留其许可并需复核。贡献者保留版权，无需转让；不得提交与雇主、合著者、出版社或数据方约定冲突的内容。

## Review and public release / 审阅与公开

Maintainers review contributions before accepting them. Contributing, sponsorship, and public-release approval are separate decisions. No payment is required for contributions or issue reports. Current public-release blockers are recorded in [RIGHTS_REVIEW.md](RIGHTS_REVIEW.md).

维护者审阅后决定收录。贡献接受、赞助和公开发布是不同事项；提问与投稿不要求付费。当前公开前待办见 [RIGHTS_REVIEW.md](RIGHTS_REVIEW.md)。
