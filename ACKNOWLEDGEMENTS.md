本课程练习册的部分练习设计与版式借鉴了 [Polaris-Aeterna/loom-notes](https://github.com/Polaris-Aeterna/loom-notes) 的 LaTeX 模板（作者：北极甜虾 / Polaris），包括其中的主动回忆、填空、证明骨架与自主练习等设计思路。本课程按自身教学目标选择和调整这些设计，并非完整套用该模板，也不意味着全部练习题、解答或课程内容均来自该项目。各章节与题目已有的其他来源说明继续保留。

Parts of this course’s workbook exercise design and layout draw on the LaTeX template in [Polaris-Aeterna/loom-notes](https://github.com/Polaris-Aeterna/loom-notes), by 北极甜虾 (Polaris), including approaches to active recall, fill-in steps, proof skeletons, and independent practice. These designs are selectively adapted to this course’s learning goals; the template is not used in full. This credit does not mean that all exercises, answers, or course content originate from that project. Existing chapter-specific and exercise-specific source credits remain in place.
