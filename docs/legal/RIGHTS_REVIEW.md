- 引用的论文、书籍、课程、模板、字体和图片保留各自许可。本项目的许可不替代其权利人的授权。
- French 等市场数据不随仓库分发。数据及衍生图表的研究、商业使用和再分发条件，以提供方条款为准。
- 部分改编练习、数据衍生图表和嵌入字体的授权依据仍在核对。需要再分发相关材料时，请先与维护者确认。

Papers, books, courses, templates, fonts and images retain their own terms. French market data are not distributed with this repository; provider terms govern research use, commercial use and redistribution of data and derived charts. Permissions for some adapted exercises, data-derived figures and embedded fonts are still being checked. Contact the maintainer before redistributing those materials.
