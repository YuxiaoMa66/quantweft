收益率与复利Returns and compounding

定义与条件DEFINITION AND CONDITIONS

先假定没有外部入金或提款，所有投资现金流都正确归入账户。简单收益定义为First assume no external deposits or withdrawals and that all investment cash flows are assigned correctly to the account. Simple return is defined as

Rₜ = (Vₜ − Vₜ₋₁) / Vₜ₋₁

V 有货币单位，Rₜ 无量纲；期初财富 Vₜ₋₁ > 0。V has currency units; Rₜ is dimensionless. Starting wealth Vₜ₋₁ > 0.

直觉：百分比的分母会变化Intuition: the percentage denominator changes

100+50%→150−50%→75

两期百分比相加为0，账户却亏25%。The two percentages sum to zero, yet the account loses 25%.
