# Project 5 research scope and limits

The c5/v2 materials describe a constructed pairs-trading study, not real-market evidence. Its primary constructed result does not support the strategy; earlier c1–c4 results use a different v1 setup and remain distinct.

The v2 design, including the eight-pair research question, training-only formation, cost and financing assumptions, and whole-share execution boundary, is described in [the research report](research_report/report.md), [preregistration](configs/preregistration_v2.json), and [execution boundary](EXECUTION_BOUNDARY.md).

Real-price research remains unavailable because data rights, permitted use, and required historical fields have not been established. The constructed examples do not establish real-pair cointegration, profitability, costs, or capacity.
